RaiderRating Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Overview

RaiderRating is a Chrome extension for Texas Tech University students. It shows Rate My Professors ratings and grade distributions on the TTU Schedule Builder, and it can copy the CRNs from your schedule into the CRN boxes on the registration page. This page explains what it reads, stores, and sends.

What We Never Collect

  • Passwords, eRaider credentials, or any login information
  • Your name, student ID, or R number
  • Your grades or registration history
  • Browsing history or activity on other websites
  • Location or financial information

We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

Ratings and Grade Data

On the TTU Schedule Builder, the extension reads the professor names and course codes shown on the page and sends them to our server to look up publicly available ratings and grade distributions. These requests do not include your name, ID, or account.

Copy and Paste Schedule

When you click Copy Schedule, the extension reads the CRNs in your Schedule Builder cart and saves them in Chrome's extension storage. When you click Paste Schedule on the registration page, it types those CRNs into the CRN boxes. It never clicks Add to Summary, never submits a form, and never registers, adds, or drops a class. The saved CRNs are never sent to our server. They stay saved until you click Clear or copy a new schedule.

Storage on Your Device

The extension stores two things in Chrome's extension storage: whether ratings are turned on, and the CRNs you copied. If you have Chrome sync turned on, Chrome may sync this storage across your own signed-in devices through your Google account. We never receive it.

Feedback Form

If you choose to send a message through Contact Us, the subject, message, and email address you type are sent to our server and delivered to the developer by email. We use them only to read and reply to your message. We do not store them in a database or add you to any list.

Server and Third-Party Services

Our server is hosted on Railway. Like any website, it sees the IP address of each request. We use it briefly in memory to limit abuse and do not save it. Our own logs record only the path and status of each request. Railway may keep standard request logs as part of hosting.

Professor ratings come from Rate My Professors' publicly available data. Feedback emails are delivered through Resend. We do not sell, trade, or share your information with anyone else.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions? Use Contact Us in the extension, or open an issue on our GitHub repository.
